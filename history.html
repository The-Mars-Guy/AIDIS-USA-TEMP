Our History

Seven Decades of Inter-American Engineering

AIDIS was born alongside the World Health Organization in 1948 — and has been the technical conscience of water and sanitation in the Americas ever since. Today, its U.S. Chapter carries that work forward as IAASEE United States.

The Inter-American Association of Sanitary and Environmental Engineering — IAASEE, known across Latin America as AIDIS — is a non-profit, technical-scientific civil society. It gathers leading institutions of professionals and students across the Americas who work in environmental preservation, health, and sanitation.

Its founding on 14 April 1948 marked a turning point for the sector: created just seven days after the founding of the World Health Organization, AIDIS has maintained broad collaboration with the WHO and the Pan American Health Organization (PAHO), ensuring recognition and representation in their assemblies and executive committees.

1948

Founded in the Americas

AIDIS is established on 14 April 1948, seven days after the World Health Organization — beginning a lasting partnership with the WHO and PAHO.

20th Century

A Continent-Wide Network

National sections are formed across the Americas, uniting engineers, scientists and public-health professionals around shared water and sanitation goals.

Permanent Seat

The Abel Wolman Headquarters

AIDIS's permanent seat — named for the pioneering sanitation engineer Abel Wolman — is located in São Paulo, where its activities are coordinated.

Today

24 National Sections

AIDIS today spans 24 national sections and roughly 32,000 members, coordinated through its technical divisions, with a footprint touching more than 32 countries of the Americas and the Caribbean.

Recent

A New Chapter

The IAASEE United States Chapter opens a new chapter, connecting American professionals to the Inter-American network.

The U.S. Chapter

Our Story in the United States

IAASEE United States exists to give American water professionals a seat at the Inter-American table — sharing research, standards and field experience with colleagues from Canada to Argentina.

The Chapter is building a U.S. network of engineers, utilities, universities, public-health agencies, and students, with a focus on technical exchange, professional development, and practical solutions that work in American communities. Chapter programs, events, and partnerships are announced through this site and the chapter inbox.

IAASEE United States is a Chapter of AIDIS, the Inter-American Association of Sanitary and Environmental Engineering.

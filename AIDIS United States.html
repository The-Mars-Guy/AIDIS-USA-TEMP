Who We Are

The Bridge Between Science and Safe Water

AIDIS United States is the U.S. chapter of the Inter-American Association of Sanitary and Environmental Engineering — a hemispheric network with over seven decades of technical leadership across 18 countries.

We bring together engineers, scientists, public health experts, and policymakers to address America's most pressing water and sanitation challenges: aging infrastructure, equitable access, climate resilience, and regulatory innovation.

As the standard-bearer for the Americas, our U.S. chapter is positioned to lead — with world-class research, rigorous technical exchange, and a commitment to solutions that reach every community.

Our Purpose

Mission & Vision

Grounded in science. Driven by equity. Built for the long-term health of people and planet.

Our Mission

To advance the science, technology, and practice of sanitary and environmental engineering in the United States — fostering technical excellence, professional development, and collaborative problem-solving to protect public health and the environment.

Our Vision

A United States where every person has access to clean water and a healthy environment — powered by world-class engineering, equitable policy, and cross-sector collaboration that sets the global standard for the Americas.

Who We Are

A U.S. Chapter with Inter-American Reach

IAASEE United States is the U.S. Chapter of AIDIS — the Inter-American Association of Sanitary and Environmental Engineering — connecting American professionals to a continent-wide community of practice.

IAASEE United States is the U.S. Chapter of the Inter-American Association of Sanitary and Environmental Engineering — known across Latin America as AIDIS.

We bring together engineers, scientists, public-health experts, utilities, and policymakers to address the nation's most pressing water and sanitation challenges: aging infrastructure, equitable access, climate resilience, and regulatory innovation.

As a chapter of the standard-bearer for the Americas, we are positioned to lead — with world-class research, rigorous technical exchange, and a commitment to solutions that reach every community.

One organization, two names

IAASEE — Inter-American Association of Sanitary and Environmental Engineering — is the English name of the organization known across Latin America as AIDIS. The IAASEE United States Chapter is the U.S. Chapter of this Inter-American association.

How We Are Different

An association built for cross-border technical exchange.

  • Inter-American, not national-only. A U.S. home base with direct access to the AIDIS network across the Americas.
  • Technical-scientific, not a trade group. We advance practice and public health, not a commercial or lobbying agenda.
  • Multidisciplinary, not engineering-only. Engineers, scientists, public-health professionals, policymakers, and students.
  • Chapter-led, not headquarters-led. U.S. priorities set by U.S. professionals, backed by Inter-American resources.

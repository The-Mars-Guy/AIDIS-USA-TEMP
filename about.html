Who We Are

A Hemispheric Network, Built for U.S. Water

The U.S. chapter of the Inter-American Association of Sanitary and Environmental Engineering — connecting American professionals to a continent-wide community of practice.

AIDIS United States is the U.S. chapter of the Inter-American Association of Sanitary and Environmental Engineering — a hemispheric network with over seven decades of technical leadership across the Americas.

We bring together engineers, scientists, public health experts, and policymakers to address the nation's most pressing water and sanitation challenges: aging infrastructure, equitable access, climate resilience, and regulatory innovation.

As a chapter of the standard-bearer for the Americas, we are positioned to lead — with world-class research, rigorous technical exchange, and a commitment to solutions that reach every community.

Our Mission

To advance the science, technology, and practice of sanitary and environmental engineering in the United States — fostering technical excellence, professional development, and collaborative problem-solving to protect public health and the environment.

Our Vision

A United States where every person has access to clean water and a healthy environment — powered by world-class engineering, equitable policy, and cross-sector collaboration that sets the global standard for the Americas.

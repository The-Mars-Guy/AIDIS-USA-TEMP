Who We Are

A U.S. Chapter with Inter-American Reach

IAASEE United States is the U.S. Chapter of AIDIS — the Inter-American Association of Sanitary and Environmental Engineering. We connect American engineers, scientists, public-health experts, utilities, and organizations to a 24-section network across the Americas.

We are a technical-scientific, non-profit chapter: a platform for peer exchange, professional development, and practical solutions to water and sanitation challenges across the region.

Why IAASEE

A U.S. Network. An Inter-American Platform.

IAASEE United States connects the people and organizations shaping water in the United States with the technical depth and reach of the AIDIS network across the Americas.

01

U.S. Professional Network

Connect with engineers, utilities, universities, consultants, public-health agencies, NGOs, and students working on drinking water, wastewater, stormwater, and environmental health across the United States.

02

Inter-American Reach

Work directly with colleagues from 24 national sections and more than 32 countries — sharing standards, research, and field experience from Canada to Argentina.

03

Technical, Not Commercial

We are a technical-scientific, non-profit association. Our work is driven by evidence, peer review, and public health — not by sales, lobbying, or a single sector's agenda.

04

Multidisciplinary by Design

Water is never one profession. Our membership spans engineering, science, public health, policy, academia, and the next generation of water leaders.

How We Are Different

An association built for cross-border technical exchange.

  • Inter-American, not national-only. A U.S. home base with direct access to the AIDIS network across the Americas.
  • Technical-scientific, not a trade group. We advance practice and public health, not commercial interests.
  • Multidisciplinary, not engineering-only. Engineers, scientists, public-health professionals, policymakers, and students.
  • Chapter-led, not headquarters-led. U.S. priorities set by U.S. professionals, backed by Inter-American resources.
